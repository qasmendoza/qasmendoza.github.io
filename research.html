Research

Research Focus

My research bridges robotics, machine learning, computer vision, formal verification, and embedded systems to create practical AI-powered solutions for agriculture and food safety. I am particularly interested in building low-cost, deployable systems that bring state-of-the-art deep learning to edge devices, making intelligent monitoring accessible where it is needed most.

Research Areas

Robotics, ROS & Embedded Systems

Designing modular robotic systems for agricultural imaging and automation using ROS 2 and Micro-ROS on resource-constrained embedded platforms. Current work focuses on multi-axis seed imaging that achieves complete 3D coverage through synchronized motor-camera control, eliminating blind spots found in single-view approaches. The architecture leverages SysML v2 model-based design for formal specification and validation, enabling real-time coordination across distributed sensor nodes with easy integration of new sensors and algorithms. Targeted improvements include reducing measurement error to <2% (vs 3% baseline) through multi-axis rotation and modular, scalable system design.

AI-Based Insect Detection for Grain Facilities

Developing real-time insect pest detection and identification systems for grain storage and food facilities. Using convolutional neural networks (CNNs) deployed on low-cost edge computing platforms (NVIDIA Jetson Nano) paired with camera systems, the goal is to enable automated, scalable integrated pest management (IPM) decisions. The system detects and classifies stored-product pests such as warehouse beetles and cigarette beetles under varying lighting conditions with greater than 85% accuracy.

Machine Learning for Agricultural Decision Support

Building data-driven recommendation systems that leverage machine learning and applied statistics to advise on optimal crop selection, fertilizer application, and disease detection. These proof-of-concept tools combine environmental sensor data, soil properties, and historical yield information to provide actionable insights for farmers and agronomists.
