Research

Research Focus

My research bridges machine learning, computer vision, and embedded systems to create practical AI-powered solutions for agriculture and food safety. I am particularly interested in building low-cost, deployable systems that bring state-of-the-art deep learning to edge devices, making intelligent monitoring accessible where it is needed most.

Research Areas

AI-Based Insect Detection for Grain Facilities

Developing real-time insect pest detection and identification systems for grain storage and food facilities. Using convolutional neural networks (CNNs) deployed on low-cost edge computing platforms (NVIDIA Jetson Nano) paired with camera systems, the goal is to enable automated, scalable integrated pest management (IPM) decisions. The system detects and classifies stored-product pests such as warehouse beetles and cigarette beetles under varying lighting conditions with greater than 85% accuracy.

Machine Learning for Agricultural Decision Support

Building data-driven recommendation systems that leverage machine learning and applied statistics to advise on optimal crop selection, fertilizer application, and disease detection. These proof-of-concept tools combine environmental sensor data, soil properties, and historical yield information to provide actionable insights for farmers and agronomists.

IoT & Remote Sensing

Exploring the integration of Internet of Things (IoT) sensor networks and remote sensing data with machine learning pipelines for environmental monitoring, precision agriculture, and cyber-physical systems. Research in this area includes developing efficient data collection, processing, and analytics workflows for sensor-rich environments.

Data Analytics & Business Intelligence
