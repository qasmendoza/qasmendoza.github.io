Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Kansas State University, working as a Robotics, Machine Learning and Data Science Graduate Research Assistant in the Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) Lab under the supervision of Dr. Mitchell Neilsen and Dr. John Hatcliff.

My research lies at the intersection of robotics, machine learning, computer vision, and IoT/embedded systems, focusing on practical AI solutions for real-world applications — from insect pest detection in grain facilities to crop recommendation systems. I am passionate about leveraging data-driven approaches to drive decision-making, optimize performance, and create tangible impact in agriculture and beyond.

Prior to academia, I spent almost a decade in IT infrastructure and operations across Hewlett-Packard Enterprise, Ingram Micro, and Accenture, where I led teams of 30+ professionals and earned multiple awards for designing solutions that improved system performance by up to 30%. I also completed a Data Science internship at Caterpillar Inc., where I developed ML models that resulted in $2M in cost savings.
